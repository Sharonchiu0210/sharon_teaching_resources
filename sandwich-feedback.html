Feedback is not only about what is true.
It is also about how we choose to say it.

回饋不只是「我說得對不對」，也包括「我選擇怎麼說」。

Rewrite the Feedback

開始練習

下面每一句都是課堂上真的會出現的話。點句型可以直接帶入欄位；三層都寫完，下面的三明治就會疊起來。

Your own
The Three Layers

三層各自在做什麼

建議放中間。上下兩片不是為了讓話好聽，是為了讓中間那句真的被聽見。

Bread

Positive

先看見對方真的做到的地方。要具體——空泛的稱讚會讓整個結構露餡。

  • I like your ideas.
  • You did a good job organizing your presentation.
Filling

Suggestion

一次只給一個，而且下一次就做得到。

  • Maybe you could speak a little louder.
  • You might add one more example here.
Bread

Encouragement

給一個往前走的理由，不是給一句安慰。

  • I believe your next draft will be even better.
  • Keep practicing. You're making progress.
Useful Expressions

可以直接用的句型

Positive

I like…

I really enjoyed…

One thing you did well was…

I can tell you…

Suggestion

Maybe you could…

You might try…

One thing to improve is…

Next time, try…

Encouragement

Keep going!

I believe you can do even better.

You're improving every time.

Good feedback is not about making criticism softer.
It's about making growth possible.

好的回饋，不是把真話包裝起來，而是讓對方願意聽見，也願意繼續前進。

這個工具只回答一個問題：How can I say it so people can hear it？
它不回答另一個——怎麼把問題說得具體，而不是評價這個人。那是另一堂課。
